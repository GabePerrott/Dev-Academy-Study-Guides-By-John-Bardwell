Your guide to the guides

Fourteen interactive guides, organised into three units that stack on top of each other. Whether you're flying or feeling completely lost, start with the panel below — it tells you where to go and what to do when something doesn't make sense yet.

Start here — how to use these

Three habits that make the difference, especially if the material feels fast.

1

The units build on each other

Unit 1 (JavaScript) holds up Unit 2 (React), which holds up Unit 3 (APIs). If a later guide feels like nonsense, the missing piece is almost always one unit down — not in the guide you're reading.

2

Poke it, don't just read it

Every guide has clickable widgets, predict-the-output cards, and checkpoints. Reading them is worth a little. Actually using them — guessing before you reveal — is worth a lot.

3

Use the 15-minute rule

Stuck on one thing for 15 minutes? That's your signal to ask — a peer, David, or Rosie. Getting stuck isn't falling behind; noticing it and asking is the process.

◆ Foundations feeling shaky? Read this first

Struggling with the basics isn't a sign you're behind everyone else — it's a sign you've found the exact thing worth working on. That's useful information, not a verdict.

The trap is pushing forward into React or APIs while objects, arrays, and functions still feel slippery. That doesn't skip the confusion — it just moves it somewhere harder to see. It's genuinely faster to go back a unit than to power through on a cracked foundation.

Smallest possible first step: open Unit 1 → Data Types, Functions & Modules, do the one typeof playground, then stop. That's a real win. Come back for the next piece when you're ready.
